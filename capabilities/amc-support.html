WHY ONE AMC, NOT FOUR

The usual pattern is a separate maintenance vendor per system — one for CCTV, one for networking, one for servers, one for cabling — each with its own contract, its own renewal date and its own number to call when something goes wrong. That's manageable at one site with in-house IT staff. It's a real liability at multiple sites without one. Bundling everything we've installed under a single AMC means one renewal date, one invoice and one team that already knows your site, rather than four vendors pointing at each other when a fault sits at the boundary between two systems.

Scheduled preventive health checks, fault response and repair, handling of spares and replacement parts, and firmware/software updates on networking and security equipment — under one contract instead of separate call-outs per issue.
