GAMING PC OR WORKSTATION?

A gaming PC is tuned for frame rate: GPU horsepower and fast single-core performance for the games you play. A workstation build is tuned differently — sustained multi-core performance, colour-accurate displays and often ECC memory for CAD, 3D and video work. If you're not sure which one your workload actually needs, that's exactly the kind of question worth asking before you buy rather than after.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
How is a custom gaming PC different from a pre-built one?
A custom build is specified around the games and frame rate you actually want, not a fixed retail configuration — every component is chosen to match, rather than sold as a package with parts you don't need.
What's the difference between a gaming PC and a workstation PC?
A gaming PC is tuned for frame rate. A workstation is tuned for sustained multi-core workloads like rendering, 3D and video export, often with ECC memory and workstation-class GPUs. We'll tell you honestly which one your workload actually needs.
Do you build gaming PCs for businesses, not just individuals?
Yes — gaming lounges, cafes and streaming setups are business builds too, and we quote them with GST billing the same way as any other order.
Do gaming PC builds come with a warranty?
Yes, every build is tested and warranted in-house before it ships, with support handled directly by us.
